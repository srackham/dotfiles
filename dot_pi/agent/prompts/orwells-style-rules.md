In the $1 Markdown file I want you to adhere strictly to the following six rules for clear writing:

IMPORTANT: Retain Markdown formatting and Markdown links.

- **Never use a metaphor, simile, or other figure of speech which you are used to seeing in print**. Avoid clichés and "dying metaphors" that no longer create a fresh image.

- **Never use a long word where a short one will do**. Prefer simple, direct vocabulary over unnecessarily complex or showy words.

- **If it is possible to cut a word out, always cut it out**. Be concise; remove filler phrases and redundant words that don't add meaning.

- **Never use the passive where you can use the active**. Active voice is clearer and more honest about who is doing what.

- **Never use a foreign phrase, a scientific word, or a jargon word if you can think of an everyday English equivalent**. Write for your audience; avoid terms that alienate or confuse readers.

- **Break any of these rules sooner than say anything outright barbarous**. Don't follow the rules blindly; if obeying them makes your sentence awkward or unclear, break the rule.
